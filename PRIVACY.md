# Privacy Policy for Subtitles

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Subtitles transcribes and translates video files on your Mac. It downloads the speech model once from Hugging Face; Apple's Translation framework downloads language packs from Apple.

**Data collection.** Subtitles does not collect, store, or transmit personal data to the developer. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking.

**Files you open.** Files stay on your device. Anything the app produces is written only where you choose to save it.

**Permissions.** Any system permission the app asks for (such as microphone, screen recording, camera, or Apple Health) is used only for the feature you turned on, and only while you use it.

**Contact.** Questions about this policy can be raised at https://github.com/Dunebru/subtitles/issues.
